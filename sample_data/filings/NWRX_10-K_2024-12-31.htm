PART I
Item 1. Business
Northwind Robotics Inc. (the “Company”) designs and manufactures warehouse automation systems, including autonomous mobile robots and fleet-management software used by logistics and e-commerce customers.
As of December 31, 2024, we employed approximately 9,956 people worldwide and operated 5 assembly facilities.
Item 1A. Risk Factors
We depend on single-source suppliers for key components. Several critical components, including lidar sensors and drive motors, are available only from single-source suppliers. A disruption at any of these suppliers could delay shipments and harm our results. Supply chain disruptions in fiscal 2024 lengthened our average component lead times by 4 weeks.
Our revenue is concentrated among a limited number of customers. In fiscal 2024, our largest customer accounted for 20.0% of total revenue, and the loss of that customer would materially reduce our revenue.
We have substantial indebtedness. As of December 31, 2024, we had $2,185 million of principal indebtedness outstanding, which could limit our flexibility to fund operations and invest in growth.
Cybersecurity incidents or failures of our information systems could disrupt operations, expose confidential information and damage our reputation.
Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Revenue. Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $5,240 million, an increase of 12.2% compared with $4,672 million in fiscal 2023, driven by higher unit volumes and pricing.
Gross margin. Gross profit was $1,908 million, or 36.4% of revenue, compared with 37.4% in fiscal 2023.
Liquidity and capital resources. As of December 31, 2024, we had cash and cash equivalents of $583 million and our revolving credit facility provided total commitments of $1,500 million, of which $375 million was drawn. The facility matures in 2028.
Net cash provided by operating activities was $734 million. Capital expenditures were 288 million in fiscal 2024, primarily for manufacturing and distribution capacity.
