PART I
Item 1. Business
Harbor Lane Foods Corp. (the “Company”) distributes packaged and refrigerated food products to grocery retailers and sells private-label products under long-term supply agreements.
As of June 30, 2024, we employed approximately 7,814 people and operated 7 distribution centers.
Item 1A. Risk Factors
Our products are perishable and spoilage could reduce our margins. Perishable inventory write-offs were $20 million in fiscal 2024, and a failure of our cold-chain logistics could result in larger losses.
Our revenue is concentrated among a limited number of customers. In fiscal 2024, our largest customer, a national grocery chain, accounted for 24.3% of total revenue, and the loss of that customer would materially reduce our revenue.
We have substantial indebtedness. As of June 30, 2024, we had $1,335 million of principal indebtedness outstanding, which could limit our flexibility to fund operations and invest in growth.
Cybersecurity incidents or failures of our information systems could disrupt operations, expose confidential information and damage our reputation.
Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Revenue. Revenue for fiscal 2024 was $3,256 million, an increase of 12.5% compared with $2,893 million in fiscal 2023, driven by higher unit volumes and pricing.
Gross margin. Gross profit was $1,238 million, or 38.0% of revenue, compared with 38.3% in fiscal 2023.
Liquidity and capital resources. As of June 30, 2024, we had cash and cash equivalents of $386 million and our revolving credit facility provided total commitments of $900 million, of which $225 million was drawn. The facility matures in 2028.
Net cash provided by operating activities was $456 million. Capital expenditures were 179 million in fiscal 2024, primarily for manufacturing and distribution capacity.
